Check your digital maps are correct

{# Content of 10 November 2026 #}

Check that the land details on your digital maps are up to date. If they’re not, you may not be able to apply for some actions or items.

Read the guidance on how to check your digital maps (opens in new tab).

What to check

Check that your digital maps show:

  • all the land parcels you want to include in this application
  • the correct total area for each land parcel, in hectares
  • the correct land covers for each land parcel - for example, arable land, permanent grassland, permanent crops or relevant non-agricultural land cover
