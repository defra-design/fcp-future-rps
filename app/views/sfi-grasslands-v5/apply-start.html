Apply for a grasslands agreement

Use this service to:

  • choose actions and capital items for your land
  • see what you could be paid before you apply

As you add actions, we’ll work out how much land is available for each one and what you could be paid. These are estimates until you get your agreement offer.

Grasslands agreements last for 3 years.

Who can apply

You can apply if:

  • your business is registered with the Rural Payments Agency and has a single business identifier (SBI)
  • you have management control of the land for 3 years
  • your land will stay eligible for 3 years
  • the actions are not already funded by another scheme
  • the actions are not something you’re already legally required to do

If you’re an agent, you need permission to apply for the business.

Before you start

You’ll sign in to the Farm and Land Service with your Rural Payments customer reference number (CRN) and password.

Check your land details are up to date in the Rural Payments service. If they’re wrong, you’ll need to update them before you can apply.

Your answers are saved as you go. You can return to your application from the Farm and Land Service.
