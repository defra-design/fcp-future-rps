Apply for a grasslands agreement

{# Wording from the user's design (10 November 2026) #}

Use this service to apply for a 3-year grassland agreement.

Who can apply

You can apply for an agreement if:

  • you will have management control of the land for the duration of the agreement
  • your land will stay eligible for the duration of the agreement
  • actions and items are not already funded by another scheme
  • actions are not things you’re legally required to do

If you’re an agent, you need permission to apply for the business.

How the service works

You’ll need to sign in using your Rural Payments customer reference number (CRN) and password.

As you add actions and items to land parcels, the service will automatically calculate:

  • how much remaining land is available
  • what you could be paid

You can save your application at any time, and return to it through the Farm and Land Service.
