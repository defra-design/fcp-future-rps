Before you submit your application

It’s your responsibility to check that land (including landscape features) is eligible for your Grasslands actions. Payments may be reduced if the RPA finds ineligible land, features or incompatible actions.

If you’ve applied for Grasslands actions on land that has a conditional exemption from Inheritance Tax, you will not be paid for activities which duplicate, or conflict with, your undertakings under that exemption.

If you’ve applied for Grasslands actions on land owned by a public body, you will not be paid for activities which conflict with, or duplicate, the public body’s statutory duties.

You must make sure that you have management control of the land for the duration of the actions.
