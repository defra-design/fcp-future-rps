1. Introduction and overview

This document sets out the details of your Grasslands agreement, including:

  • the land, actions and items covered by your agreement
  • the payments you’ll receive

Your Grasslands agreement consists of:

  • your Grasslands agreement
  • the Grasslands terms and conditions (opens in a new tab)
  • the actions you have selected

By accepting this agreement, you agree to comply with the grasslands terms and conditions and the requirements for your selected actions.

You should read these documents carefully so you understand what you must do for the duration of your agreement.
