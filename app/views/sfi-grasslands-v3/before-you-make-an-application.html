Before you make an application

Before you apply, check if any of these requirements apply to your land.

Land eligibility

Make sure your land will be eligible for the duration of your agreement.

Historic or archaeological features

If you select actions on land with historic or archaeological features, you’ll need SFI Historic Environment Farm Environment Record (HEFER) (opens in a new tab). You must have this consent before you apply.

Sites of special scientific interest (SSSI)

If you select actions on SSSI land, you’ll need SSSI consent (opens in a new tab) from Natural England. You must have this consent before you apply.

Inheritance tax exemptions and maintenance funds

Check that the actions you select are allowed if any of your land:

  • has a conditional exemption from inheritance tax
  • is the object of a maintenance fund

Read the guidance on eligible funding (opens in a new tab).

Other funding or legal requirements

You must not apply for actions which include activities that:

  • you are legally required to carry out
  • are already funded by other sources
