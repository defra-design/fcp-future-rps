Before you make an application

Some of these only apply depending on your land and the actions you choose.

  • make sure your land remains eligible for the duration of your agreement
  • if you apply for actions on land with historic or archaeological features, get an SFI Historic Environment Farm Environment Record (SFI HEFER) (opens in new tab) from Historic England. You must not start the actions until you have your HEFER
  • if you select actions on SSSI land, get site of special scientific interest (SSSI) consent (opens in new tab) from Natural England. You must have this before the RPA can offer you an agreement
  • if any of your land has a conditional exemption from inheritance tax or is the object of a maintenance fund, check your selected actions are allowed on that land. Read the guidance on eligible funding (opens in new tab)
  • not apply for actions that include activities you are legally required to carry out or are already funded by other sources
