Before you make an application

You confirm you will:

  • make sure your land remains eligible for the duration of your agreement
  • get an SFI Historic Environment Farm Environment Record (SFI HEFER) from Historic England if you apply for actions on land with historic or archaeological features — you must have this in place before the RPA can offer you an agreement
  • get site of special scientific interest (SSSI) consent from Natural England if you select to do actions on SSSI land — you must not start doing these actions until you have your SSSI consent
  • check if you can do your selected actions on your land if you have land with a conditional exemption from inheritance tax or maintenance fund
  • not apply for actions that include activities you are legally required to carry out or are already funded by other sources
