Before you make an application

Before you apply, check whether any of these requirements apply to your land.

  • Land eligibility – make sure your land remains eligible for the duration of your agreement.
  • Historic or archaeological features – if you apply for actions on land with historic or archaeological features, get a Historic Environment Farm Environment Record (HEFER) (opens in new tab) from Historic England. You must have your HEFER before you start the relevant actions.
  • Sites of special scientific interest (SSSI) – if you select actions on SSSI land, get SSSI consent (opens in new tab) from Natural England. You must have consent before the RPA can offer you an agreement.
  • Inheritance tax exemptions and maintenance funds – if any of your land has a conditional exemption from inheritance tax or is the object of a maintenance fund, check that your selected actions are allowed on that land. Read the guidance on eligible funding (opens in new tab).
  • Other funding or legal requirements – do not apply for actions that include activities you are legally required to carry out or that are already funded by other sources.
