Accept your agreement offer

Your agreement will consist of:

  • your Grasslands agreement
  • the Grasslands terms and conditions (opens in a new tab)
  • the actions you have selected

By accepting this offer, you confirm on your own behalf or, where applicable, on behalf of the agreement holder, that:

  • you will comply with the obligations under your agreement
  • the information you gave and the declarations you made in your application are true and accurate to the best of your knowledge and belief
  • you have the authority to enter into the agreement
  • you are not aware of any circumstances that would make you ineligible or prevent you from meeting your obligations under the agreement
  • your obligations under this agreement do not duplicate or conflict with any other legal or contractual obligations you are subject to
  • you will comply with all relevant laws when carrying out your obligations under the agreement

Read the Grasslands terms and conditions (opens in a new tab) to understand what can happen if you deliberately give wrong or incomplete information, or do not report changes as soon as possible.

If you are accepting this offer on behalf of the agreement holder, you also confirm that you have the authority to:

  • accept this agreement on the agreement holder’s behalf
  • enter into the agreement on the agreement holder’s behalf
