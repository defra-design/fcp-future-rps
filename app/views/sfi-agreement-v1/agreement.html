1. Introduction and overview

This is the Sustainable Farming Incentive (SFI) agreement document. It describes the payment (the “Grant”) you will receive for delivering the SFI action(s) you have chosen.

The SFI “Agreement” comprises:

  • this SFI agreement document (“Agreement Document”)
  • the Grasslands terms and conditions (opens in a new tab) (“Terms and Conditions”)
  • the Grasslands actions for 2026 (opens in a new tab) you have chosen (“Actions”)

When you enter the Agreement, you are agreeing to the terms applicable to the payment of the Grant. You should review the mandatory requirements of your Agreement to make sure you understand the full extent of your obligations under the Agreement.
