SSSI and HEFER on select actions

Two different rules, shown differently. Do not use “Requires…” for actions that are actually ineligible.

Rule

  • Ineligible on SSSI / historic features → deduct that area from this action’s available quantity + short “Not eligible…” hint
  • Eligible but needs consent / HEFER → keep available quantity + “Requires…” hint (no deduction)
{# ——— Pattern cards ——— #}

1. The two patterns

Ineligible → deduct

You cannot enter the designated area into this action. Remaining parcel area can still be used.

Example: parcel grassland 33.66 ha − 4 ha SSSI − 1 ha historic = 28.66 ha for CSAM3.

Eligible → requires (no deduct)

Action is allowed on that land, but consent / HEFER must be in place before you do it.

Same grassland pool as other eligible actions — no SSSI/HEFER cut.
